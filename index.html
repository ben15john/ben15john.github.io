Featured work · Client advisory case study

Supporting client on ERP reconciliation.

This is an example of a real scenario I worked on during my internship supporting clients. This scenario saw me being able to diagnose the issue and guide the client to a solution.

Client issue

The client was attempting to complete a balance swap within their ERP system however there was a discrepancy between the balances in their books and the balances in their bank and investment accounts. The client needed to understand this discrepancy to decide what to do.

Investigation

I took ownership of the request and began to investigate. By looking at the transactions in both the bank and books I was able to identify where the discrepancy occurred. I then used documentation, previous support cases, a colleague’s knowledge and my own testing to understand how to resolve the discrepancies.

Presenting solutions.

During a meeting with the client I presented the findings and went through both options that had been considered. We discussed how going back to the period of the original error would be the best option or considering a journal entry. We talked about how this would impact their audits. Ultimately the decision to resolve the issue would come down to the accounting team.

Client decision

The client decided to go back to the date of the error and correct it. I guided them through the process in ERP. I helped them to make an informed decision on a complicated system issue.

Consultative leadership

Ownership doesn’t mean knowing everything, it means thoroughly researching, presenting the options and trade-offs and guiding the client to make an informed decision.
